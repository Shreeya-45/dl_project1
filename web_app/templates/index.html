Capstone Project Details & Team Responsibilities

Department of Electronics Engineering | Ramdeobaba University, Nagpur

Course: Deep Learning Lab (Session 2026–27)
Course Coordinator: Prof. V. R. Gupta
Dataset Sources: FloodNet + CVFD + Flood Detection v1

Project Objectives

  1. To Develop a unified multi-dataset training pipeline combining FloodNet with CVFD and public datasets.
  2. To Train a lightweight Deep Learning segmentation model (MobileNet-UNet) for pixel-level flood and road identification.
  3. To Estimate road passability using flooded-road pixel ratio and connected road geometry into Passable, Partially Flooded, or Severely Affected.
  4. To Evaluate the complete system quantitatively using mIoU, Dice Score, flooded-road IoU, passability F1-score, boundary quality, FPS, and latency.
